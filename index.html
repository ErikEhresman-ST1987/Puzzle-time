Lights Out

Tap a square to toggle its light and its neighbors. Turn them all off!

Moves: 0
✦ ✧ ✦
YOU DID IT!Puzzle complete — every light is off.
Tap a light to begin.
✦ A simple strategy

Start at the top. Look for a light that is on, then tap the square directly below it. Work across the row, then move down to the next row. This clears lights above you. The bottom row may take another try—use Restart and try a different beginning. Remember, tapping the same square twice cancels that move.

Each tap changes the selected square and its up, down, left, and right neighbors. Hint highlights a square you can press. No timer or move limit.

Tiny Railway Dispatcher

Choose a route through the countryside and guide the train to its station.

Destination: Sunshine Station
✦ ✧ ✦
YOU DID IT!The train arrived at Sunshine Station!
Set your switches and run the train.
✦ A simple strategy

Start at the green destination marker and follow the tracks backward toward the train. Follow the green pointers at each switch to choose the route, then press Run Train. If you end up at the wrong stop, adjust the switches and try again!

New Railway makes a fresh variation of our miniature track networks. Difficulty measures how many switch changes are needed from the starting setup.
