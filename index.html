✦ A simple strategy

Start at the top. Look for a light that is on, then tap the square directly below it. Work across the row, then move down to the next row. This clears lights above you. The bottom row may take another try—use Restart and try a different beginning. Remember, tapping the same square twice cancels that move.

Each tap changes the selected square and its up, down, left, and right neighbors. Hint highlights a square you can press. No timer or move limit.

✦ A little Sudoku strategy

Start with a row, column, or 2×3 box that has several numbers filled in. Work out which numbers are missing, then check the crossing row, column, and box to eliminate possibilities.

If only one number fits a square, you have a single. Sometimes a number has only one possible place in a whole row, column, or box, even when several numbers could fit that square. That is a hidden single.

On tougher puzzles, pencil marks can help you track possibilities. The Hint button points to a logical deduction without entering a number for you.

Tap a cell, then a number. Given numbers cannot be changed. Completed rows, columns, and boxes light up! New Puzzle creates a fresh variation of the playtested difficulty. More advanced difficulty calibration comes next.
